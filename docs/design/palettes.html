Supplement Store Palettes

Three palettes from the design research, applied to Sam's pay page and Dr. Rivera's portal. The fonts are the same in all three, so only the colours change. All names, the practice and the phone number are made up, and every amount follows the agreed money rules: a 0.75% fee, rounded up per bottle. We chose A. The research first picked C for its look, and the colour-blindness check below is why we moved.

View the mocks with

More contrast is what the app switches to when a device asks for it: secondary text turns to body text, and light borders darken. The high-contrast view approximates a dark Windows contrast theme. With a real contrast theme on, the browser replaces every colour itself, and these mocks keep their borders, focus outlines, words and icons. Press Tab inside any phone or portal to walk its keyboard order.

Contrast, computed on this page

WCAG 2.2 ratios for the standard view, rounded down. Text needs 4.5:1. Input borders and focus rings need 3:1.

Colour blindness, computed on this page

How different two colours look, as a CIE76 colour difference, after simulating the two most common types of red-green colour blindness (Machado 2009). Higher is easier to tell apart. Red and green statuses drop from about 100 to between 15 and 27 in every palette, which is why each status also carries a word and an icon.
